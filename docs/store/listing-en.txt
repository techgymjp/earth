"Find Brazil!" "Find Croatia!"
Spin a realistic 3D globe, find the country you're asked for, and tap it. Find the Country is a world geography quiz for anyone who wants to learn where countries are — map lovers, travelers, and anyone brushing up on geography.

■ How to play
- Drag to spin the globe, pinch to zoom
- Found the country? Tap it!
- Miss three times and you're out of hearts — the answer is shown
- Every wrong tap tells you which country you hit, so you learn the world map as you play

■ 10-question time attack
- Every answer is timed. The faster you find it, the higher your score
- Hints (which region → roughly where) and a skip button
- See your correct answers, total time and rank on the results screen. Your best score is saved

■ Three difficulty levels
- Easy: borders are shown, well-known countries
- Normal: no borders, well-known to mid-sized countries
- Hard: no borders, obscure countries such as the Baltic states and island nations in the Caribbean and the Pacific

■ Practice by region
Choose World, Asia, Europe, Africa, North America, South America or Oceania — great for focusing on the regions you don't know yet.

■ A realistic Earth floating in space
- A lifelike globe based on NASA satellite imagery
- Look at the Earth from any angle against a starry sky
- Space-themed music and sound effects

■ Also
- About 200 countries and territories, including tiny ones like Vatican City and Tuvalu
- Share your score with friends
- Out of hearts? Watch an ad to get one heart back (up to 3 times per game)
- Play offline (ads and sharing need a connection)
- English and Japanese
